# CFIR attribution and distribution

Status: source and attribution review, 12 September 2026. This document identifies a route to a future attributed CFIR asset. It does not bundle definitions, change a study codebook, or declare a release ready.

## Verified sources

The [2022 CFIR update](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s13012-022-01245-0) identifies Table 3 as its domain/construct definitions and lists Additional file 6 for short definitions and detailed descriptions. Its Rights and permissions section states CC BY 4.0 and retains exceptions for separately credited third-party material. This provides a documented source/licence basis for preparing an attributed asset; the exact selected content still needs an asset-level record.

The [CFIR tools page](https://cfirguide.org/cfir-tools-templates) lists an editable definitions workbook updated June 2025. Its [download page](https://cfirguide.org/downloads/cfir-constructs-definitions) requests contact information and exposes a download link. The inspected pages did not establish a specific redistribution licence for that workbook. The binary was not inspected in this review, so its content, notices and equivalence to the 2022 publication remain unresolved. This is an evidence gap, not a finding that the workbook cannot be distributed.

The [2025 CFIR User Guide](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s13012-025-01450-7) has a CC BY 4.0 article notice and lists coding/rating guidance and templates as supplements. They are separately versioned candidates; the article notice does not prove that a later website download is byte-identical or has no additional notice.

## Practical asset preparation

Prepare a new versioned asset from an explicitly selected primary publication source. Preserve its title, authors, DOI/source URL, source locator, retrieval date, content hash, licence and any credit lines. Inspect the actual table/supplement before extraction, retain its domain/hierarchy distinctions, and record each formatting or substantive change. Freeze the resulting asset hash and conversion method. A future current-website asset can be a separate version after its own provenance and notices are checked.

Keep CFIR attribution separate from the software's MIT licence. A licence on QualFrame code does not replace the notice attached to third-party definitions. Under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), reuse should credit the source, link the licence, disclose modifications, avoid suggesting endorsement and preserve applicable notices. The licence also restricts imposing additional legal or technological barriers on the licensed material.

An asset notice can follow this template, completed only with verified facts:

> CFIR source: Damschroder LJ, Reardon CM, Opra Widerquist MA, Lowery J (2022), *The updated Consolidated Framework for Implementation Research based on user feedback*, Implementation Science 17:75. Source: https://doi.org/10.1186/s13012-022-01245-0. Licence: CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/. Selected material: [verified locator]. Changes: [actual changes, including format conversion]. This QualFrame representation does not imply author or CFIR Leadership Team endorsement.

Use the source's exact displayed creator names in the final notice and include supplied copyright or exception notices. The template above is documentation, not evidence that conversion has happened. Do not put an invented asset checksum or a generic “no changes” statement into a release.

## Study use and public packaging are distinct

Authorization to upload a study's codebook for approved analysis governs that workflow. It does not establish the distribution provenance or licence of a public asset made from that workbook. Keep existing study codebooks and their frozen outputs unchanged; a new attributed public asset must receive a new identity and must not silently replace them.

Before bundling, verify the selected file/content notices, complete the attribution/change record, check hierarchy/content integrity, and include that asset in the reviewed release inventory. No definitions were redistributed or automatically added to the product by this review. No contact form was submitted and no additional permission request was sent.
